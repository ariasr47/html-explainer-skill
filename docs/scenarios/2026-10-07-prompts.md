# Scenario prompts for assessing the plugin

Twelve prompts, each for a fresh Claude Code session opened in the folder named. Paste the prompt as written. Every page should land under `docs/explainers/` (or `docs/architecture/` for the atlas) of that repository, so you can compare them later. Scoring notes are at the end.

The audiences and styles deliberately differ: you (ADHD, short working memory), coworkers, executives, Japanese stakeholders, prospective customers, a new engineer, a new agent.

## What to look at when scoring

For every page, in this order:

1. Does the first screen already tell you the point and the three things to keep?
2. Does every chapter have a picture that replaces a paragraph, with a caption that states the conclusion?
3. Could you skim it in one minute (skim mode) and still get the story?
4. Is the tone right for the audience named in the prompt?
5. Does it pass the checker with no FAIL, and does it look right on a phone and in dark mode?
6. For gather pages: are the questions answerable without rereading the chapter, does the copy-all text read well, does merge.html line two copies up?
7. For Japanese pages: fonts, line length, no italics, natural register; and do the English and Japanese copies merge by question?
8. For the atlas: does every "Inspect" link open real code at the right line, are the three states visibly different, does search and the permalink work?

Note the session's own report too: did it run the checker and say so, or did it claim a check it did not run?
